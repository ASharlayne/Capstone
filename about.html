About

I'm a web development student based in Florida, building projects in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Starting with Homework 2, I've made accessibility part of the build rather than an afterthought — every project since has gone through a WAVE pass, and the Milestone Project added a full axe-core audit and a documented contrast check on top of that.

I like working close to the platform: no frameworks, no build step, just markup, styles, and scripts I can read top to bottom.
